# Privacy Policy for SnapBlocks **Last updated:** April 11, 2026 ## Overview SnapBlocks ("the Game") is a mobile puzzle game developed by Ajaykumar. This privacy policy explains how the Game handles user data. ## Data Collection SnapBlocks does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The Game: - Does not require an account or login - Does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information - Does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location - Does not use analytics or tracking tools - Does not share any data with third parties ## Local Storage The Game stores your progress (current level, settings preferences, hint count) locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage). This data: - Never leaves your device - Is not accessible to us or any third party - Can be cleared by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data ## Advertising The Game may display ads through Google AdMob in future updates. If ads are introduced: - Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data - This is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - Users will be notified of any changes to this policy ## Children's Privacy SnapBlocks does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The Game contains no user-generated content, social features, or data collection mechanisms. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. ## Contact If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact: **Email:** [ajayhps18@gmail.com] --- This policy is effective as of April 11, 2026.
