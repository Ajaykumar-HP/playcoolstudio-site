Privacy Policy for SnapBlocks

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Overview

SnapBlocks ("the Game") is a mobile puzzle game developed by PlayCool Studio. This privacy policy explains how the Game handles user data.

Data Collection

SnapBlocks does not require an account or login and does not directly collect names, email addresses, contact information, camera data, microphone data, contacts, or precise location.

Local Storage

The Game stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device, such as current level, settings, hint count, and related game progress.

This local gameplay data is stored on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data.

Advertising

SnapBlocks displays ads using Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use certain data such as device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information needed to provide, measure, and improve ads.

AdMob data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children's Privacy

SnapBlocks is intended for a general casual puzzle audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
